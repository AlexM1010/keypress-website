YOUR INFORMATION

Website privacy.

Effective date: 12 October 2026

This notice covers keypress.download and its interactive browser demo. For the desktop application, read the KeyPress application privacy policy.

The demo stays in your browser

The demo runs against the example fields on this website. It does not send email, access your desktop applications, record your keyboard or screen, or call Anthropic. Text entered into the demo fields is used to display your draft on the page.

If you save a macro or subflow, its name, steps, values and any text you include are stored in your browser's localStorage for this website. The bundled showcase macro is also saved there automatically. This information is not uploaded to the KeyPress publisher. Browser storage persists until you delete it or your browser clears it. Avoid entering passwords or confidential information, particularly on a shared device.

You can delete saved macros in the editor's Macros library. To remove all saved macros, subflows and preferences, clear site data for keypress.download in your browser settings. Reset example resets the canvas and demo fields; it does not clear your saved library. Download saved flow creates a JSON file on your device; you control whether you share it.

Hosting and fonts

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website loads DM Sans and Space Grotesk through Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's font servers, which receive your IP address and request information such as your browser's user agent. The website sets a no-referrer policy. See Google Fonts privacy information.

The website does not add advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Local browser storage is used for demo functionality. External links, including the hosting privacy documentation and any future Microsoft Store link, take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email alex@keypress.download. If you contact the publisher, the email address, message and any attachments you provide are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive macro content unnecessarily.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if the website's handling of information changes. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
