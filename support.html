SUPPORT

Keypress support

Get help

Email alex@keypress.download. Say which version of Keypress you have (Settings shows it) and what you were doing, and attach the macro if it is part of the problem. Please do not send passwords or other secrets; a macro's saved secrets stay on your computer and are not in the file.

Buying and your licence

  • Free plan: Keypress allows up to three saved macros, with no time limit on editing or running them. Deleting one frees a place. A licence or active subscription allows unlimited macros.
  • Buying: use the Buy or Subscribe buttons inside Keypress. They open a Stripe checkout in your browser, and Keypress unlocks by itself when the payment finishes. Buying from inside the app is what links the purchase to your copy of Keypress.
  • Cancelling a subscription: use the link in your Stripe receipt. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: see the terms; email us with the address you used at checkout.

Common questions

  • A macro clicks in the wrong place. Run it with "Record screens" on in the Test run panel, then use Fix with Claude or re-pick the target. Steps that find a target by its name or text survive window moves; fixed coordinates do not.
  • A hotkey does nothing. Another application may own that key combination. Choose a different one on the Start step.
  • Stopping a macro. Press the kill switch shown in Settings, or Stop in the toolbar.
